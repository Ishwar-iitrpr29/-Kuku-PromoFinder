## Part 2: Quality Check — Where the Tool Picks Badly & Guardrails Added

### `FAIL-01-RECAP-DUPLICATION`: Cross-Episode Cliffhanger Recap Stitch Trap (Episodes [1, 2])
- **Naive Window Picked:** `Ep 1 (01:57.00-02:18.00) + Ep 2 (00:00.00-00:37.10)` (`58.1s`) — Naive Score: `94.2`
- **Why Naive Logic Picked It:** Both windows have maximum emotional intensity tags ('Scornful', 'Crying Out', 'Shattered') and adjacent continuity notes, tempting an LLM or sliding-window stitcher to join Ep 1's ending directly into Ep 2's opening.
- **Why It Fails on Video:** Microdramas re-stage the last 20-35 seconds of Episode N at the start of Episode N+1 for viewer retention. In Ep 1 (01:59-02:11) Gerald says 'I cannot marry a woman of such plainness... when Clarissa is the true jewel', and in Ep 2 (00:00-00:30) he repeats 'I cannot marry you, Juliana. You are far too plain...'. Joining them creates a repetitive 58-second clip where the groom rejects her twice with the same insult.
- **Engineering Fix:** Added Jaccard lexical overlap detection (`pacing_analyzer.py`) between Episode N's final 35s and Episode N+1's first 40s. When stitching across contiguous episodes, the engine automatically skips the recap zone (jumping straight to 01:14.20 in Ep 2).

### `FAIL-02-KEYWORD-EXPOSITION-ILLUSION`: High-Stakes Keyword / Zero-Visual Motion Trap (Episodes [25])
- **Naive Window Picked:** `Ep 25 (01:06.20-01:45.20)` (`39.0s`) — Naive Score: `89.5`
- **Why Naive Logic Picked It:** Dialogue contains lethal thriller keywords ('dying', 'barely breathing', 'Poison.', 'Arsenic.') paired with intense emotion tags ('Chilling', 'Horrified', 'Fury'). Text-only LLM scoring ranks this in the top 5%.
- **Why It Fails on Video:** On video (`**CAMERA:** Medium profile tracking shot as Juliana begins walking slowly along the trail`), nothing visually arresting happens in the first 3 seconds—it is just two people in dark cloaks walking and talking in a dim forest for 39 seconds. On TikTok/Reels where 70%+ of users scroll the first 2 seconds visually before processing dialogue, talking-head forest walks suffer massive 3-second drop-off.
- **Engineering Fix:** Decoupled `visual_kinetic_score` from dialogue keywords in `parser.py`: requires a physical action beat, prop reveal, or high-contrast visual confrontation in the opening 3.5 seconds (such as Ep 25 00:13.20-00:25.80 where Alexander pulls back her torn cloak to reveal a bloody riding-crop welt).

### `FAIL-03-LORE-GATED-CONFUSION`: Late-Season Lore Overload (Fails Standalone Cold-Viewer Test) (Episodes [46])
- **Naive Window Picked:** `Ep 46 (01:02.00-01:52.00)` (`50.0s`) — Naive Score: `88.1`
- **Why Naive Logic Picked It:** High conflict density: the High Priest shouts 'Crown seizure!', Alexander demands a trial, and emotion tags read ('Imperious', 'Commanding', 'Defiant').
- **Why It Fails on Video:** The entire 50-second argument hinges on 19th-century ecclesiastical bureaucracy ('no clerk in London entered your name in the Royal register', 'ten-day ecclesiastical council review'). A cold viewer scrolling Instagram who has never seen Episodes 1-45 has no idea who the High Priest is or why a marriage license paperwork dispute matters.
- **Engineering Fix:** Added a `standalone_clarity` scorer in `promo_finder.py` that rewards universally legible visual archetypes (Altar Betrayal, Shackled Bride, Planted Theft, Branding Iron) and penalizes bureaucratic/lore-heavy terms (`CONFUSING_LORE_SIGNALS`).

### `FAIL-04-PREMATURE-EMOTIONAL-RESOLUTION`: Cathartic Resolution Cut (Kills Click-Through Urgency) (Episodes [2])
- **Naive Window Picked:** `Ep 2 (01:14.20-01:50.00)` (`35.8s`) — Naive Score: `87.4`
- **Why Naive Logic Picked It:** Contains Juliana's most eloquent speech ('Do not mistake my heartbreak for your victory, Gerald... You have shown them exactly what your word is worth').
- **Why It Fails on Video:** It ends at 01:50.00 on `Juliana breathes with steady resolve, holding her head high as the solemn orchestral score swells into the final beat.` Because Juliana wins the verbal argument and the music resolves, the viewer feels satisfied and keeps scrolling instead of clicking the app link to see what happens next!
- **Engineering Fix:** Added `RESOLUTION_PENALTY_CUES` (`resolving chord`, `steady resolve`, `peaceful`) to the cliffhanger scorer, forcing the cut point either to stop on Gerald's stunned face (01:36.80) or jump-cut into Ep 3 (00:38.00) where the Matriarch orders the guards to seize her.

### `FAIL-05-SPEAKER-COLLISION-FRANKEN-STITCH`: VLM Speaker-Tag Collision Stitch ('BLOND NOBLEMAN' Bug) (Episodes [2, 41])
- **Naive Window Picked:** `Ep 2 (00:34.20-00:47.60) + Ep 41 (00:13.00-00:34.00)` (`34.4s`) — Naive Score: `85.0`
- **Why Naive Logic Picked It:** An entity-based trailer builder searching for 'Father vs. Daughter betrayal arc' queries blocks where speaker == `BLOND NOBLEMAN`.
- **Why It Fails on Video:** Because Kuku's VLM script generator labeled characters by visual appearance when unnamed, `BLOND NOBLEMAN` is Juliana's elderly father (Lord Ashcroft) in Ep 2, but in Ep 41-44 `BLOND NOBLEMAN` is the villainous cousin Rupert standing at the altar with Catherine! Stitching them together under a 'Her Own Father Put Her in Chains' hook is factually wrong on video because two completely different actors appear.
- **Engineering Fix:** Built Module 3A (`entity_resolver.py`) as a pre-processing pass before promo search, disambiguating `BLOND NOBLEMAN` by episode context (Ep 2/9 -> `LORD ASHCROFT`, Ep 27-44 -> `LORD RUPERT ASHBOURNE`).
